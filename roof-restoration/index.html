Roof Restoration in South East Melbourne

A full restoration puts an old tile roof back in working order, not just back in colour. We fix what keeps the water out first — tiles and ridge — and coat it last.

Call 0451 174 122 Free written quote

Most of the work on a tile roof happens where you can't see it from the driveway. A restoration is the whole job done in the right order: inspect, clean, replace broken tiles, re-bed and re-point the ridge, then seal and coat.

You get the owners on the roof, and a quote itemised in writing before anything starts — materials, labour and days on site, each on its own line.

How we do it

  1. Inspect and mark. We walk the roof and mark every cracked and slipped tile before anything else happens.
  2. High-pressure clean. Moss and lichen hold water against the tile and creep up under the laps. We wash before we price repairs, because half the damage is invisible under years of growth.
  3. Replace broken tiles. A field tile repair is mechanical: lift the tile above, unhook the broken one, hook the new one back on the batten. No silicone over cracks.
  4. Strip and re-bed the ridge. Every ridge cap comes off, the old bed is chipped back to the tile, and fresh sand-and-cement mortar goes down both sides. Each cap is set back to a string line so the ridge runs straight.
  5. Flexible pointing. Flexible pointing compound goes over the bed joint, both sides, the full length of the ridge. It moves with the roof where rigid mortar cracks.
  6. Prime and coat. One coat of primer or sealer soaks into the tile, then two coats of membrane. Colour is the finish, not the job.

Signs you need it

  • Cracked or crumbling mortar along the ridge
  • Moss, lichen or black growth across the tiles
  • Faded, porous tiles that stay dark long after rain
  • Cracked or slipped tiles
  • Water stains on the ceiling or in the roof space
