What We Do

Our Services

From full restorations to minor repairs, we handle every aspect of your roof with precision and care.

01

Full Roof Restoration

Complete roof makeover including cleaning, repointing, priming, and painting with premium Dulux coatings. Adds years of life to your roof.

02

Tile Replacement

Cracked, broken, or missing tiles? We source matching tiles and replace them seamlessly to maintain your roof's integrity and appearance.

03

Repointing & Bedding

Crumbling mortar and loose ridge caps? We re-bed and repoint with flexible pointing compounds to keep everything secure.

04

High-Pressure Cleaning

We blast away years of dirt, moss, and lichen build-up with professional high-pressure cleaning equipment, revealing the roof underneath.

05

Roof Painting

Transform your home's curb appeal with a fresh coat of premium roof paint. Choose from a wide range of modern colours.

06

Leak Detection & Repair

Got a leak? We'll find the source fast and fix it right the first time. No band-aid solutions — just permanent repairs.
