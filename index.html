How A Restoration Actually Works

Most of the work on a tile roof happens where you can't see it from the driveway. Drag the model around and step through the whole job — broken tile out, ridge caps off, old bed chipped back, fresh mortar, flexible pointing, then the coat.

Head lap
75 mmThe hidden overlap where each tile sits on the one below. That overlap is what keeps water out — not the tile itself.
Batten gauge
330 mmCentre to centre, so every course sits on the same line. Varies with the tile profile.
Ridge bedding
40–60 mmFresh sand and cement under every cap, both sides of the ridge. This is the part that fails first on an old roof.
Coats
1 + 2One coat of primer or sealer, then two coats of membrane. Colour is the last thing that happens, not the job.
