Roof Painting & Sealing

A good-looking roof that still leaks isn't a good roof. We only coat once the tiles and ridge underneath are right — then primer and two coats of membrane.

Call 0451 174 122 Free written quote

Colour is the last thing that happens. If the tiles and ridge aren't right underneath, all you've bought is a nice colour on a roof that still lets water in.

So painting comes after the clean and repairs: one coat of primer or sealer that soaks into the porous tile, then two coats of membrane over the top.

How we do it

  1. Clean. The roof is high-pressure cleaned so the coating has a sound surface to bond to.
  2. Repair. Broken tiles replaced and the ridge re-bedded and re-pointed where it needs it.
  3. Prime or seal. One coat of primer or sealer soaks into the tile.
  4. Two coats of membrane. Two coats of membrane in the colour you choose.

Signs you need it

  • Faded or patchy tiles
  • Porous tiles that stay dark and damp long after rain
  • You're selling, or want to change the look of the house
