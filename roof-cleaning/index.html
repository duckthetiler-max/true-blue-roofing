Roof Pressure Cleaning

Moss and lichen hold water against the tile and creep up under the laps. A proper clean gets rid of them and shows what the roof really looks like underneath.

Call 0451 174 122 Free written quote

We blast away years of dirt, moss and lichen with professional high-pressure cleaning equipment.

On a restoration we always wash before we price the repairs, because half the damage is invisible under years of growth.

How we do it

  1. Check the roof first. We walk the roof and note broken tiles before cleaning, so nothing gets worse under the water.
  2. High-pressure clean. The whole roof is cleaned, ridge to gutter.
  3. Report what we found. Anything the clean uncovers — cracks, perished pointing, slipped tiles — you hear about before we touch it.

Signs you need it

  • Green, black or orange growth on the tiles
  • Moss building up along laps and valleys
  • Gutters filling with grit from the roof
