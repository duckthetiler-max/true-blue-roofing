Broken Roof Tile Replacement

Cracked, broken or slipped tiles let water past the overlap that keeps your roof dry. We replace them properly — not with a smear of silicone.

Call 0451 174 122 Free written quote

Tiles aren't glued or sealed. They hook over the battens and keep water out by overlap alone: the 75 mm head lap where each tile sits on the one below. One broken tile breaks that lap.

We source matching tiles and replace them so they sit in the roof like the originals.

How we do it

  1. Find every broken tile. We walk the roof and mark cracked, broken and slipped tiles, not just the one you can see.
  2. Free the lap. The tile above is lifted to free the overlap.
  3. Out with the old. The broken tile is unhooked off the batten and slid out.
  4. In with the new. The replacement slides up, hooks its nib over the batten, and the lap goes back over it.

Signs you need it

  • Visible cracks or chips in tiles
  • Tiles that have slipped out of line
  • Daylight visible in the roof space
  • A damp patch after rain in one spot

Roof Tile Replacement questions

Sealing a cracked tile with silicone hides the problem; it doesn't fix it. A field tile repair is mechanical: the broken tile comes out and a sound one goes in.
We look for a matching tile for every replacement. If an exact match isn't available, we talk you through the closest option before we fit it.
